Privacy
policy.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how A1 FAMILY MEDIA handles information when you use the A1 FAMILY MEDIA Android app or visit this website.

1. The app and your media

A1 FAMILY MEDIA is an Android music and video player. It requests access to audio and video on your device so it can find and play your local media. Android may offer full or selected media access depending on your device and Android version. You can review or change these permissions in Android Settings.

Your audio and video files are played from your device. The player does not need to upload the media files themselves to provide its local playback features.

2. Lyrics and exported files

Lyrics that you write or paste in the app are saved locally on your device for the selected track. The app's lyrics feature does not send the lyrics text to the A1 analytics service. If you choose to export a PDF or share a file, that action is under your control and the destination app or service will handle the file under its own terms.

The app also saves optional personalisation on your device, including theme colours, wallpaper and home-screen layout preferences, saved theme and equalizer profiles, folder labels, and track-memory notes or mood selections. If you attach a photo to a track memory, the app stores a reference to the photo you selected so it can display it later; it does not upload that photo to the A1 analytics service. These personalisation details are stored locally in the app and are not included in the optional installation analytics event.

3. App analytics

Optional installation analytics is offered only in app builds where the analytics key is configured. Before the first installation event is sent, the app explains the information involved and asks you to choose whether to allow it. If you decline, the installation event is not sent and this choice does not affect playback or advertising. You can review or change this choice later from the Privacy & data section on the app's Home screen. If you allow it, the app sends a first-open event to the A1 Family Cloud API. The event includes a randomly generated app-install identifier, a unique event identifier, the event time, the app version and the Android platform. These identifiers are pseudonymous; they are not your name or email address. The app sends this event once per installation after the server accepts it. If you later decline, the app stops future optional analytics sends, but this does not retract an event already received by the service. If the app's analytics key is not configured, no analytics prompt or event is shown or sent.

The API stores the event details in a Cloudflare D1 database so the app publisher can count first app launches and understand which app versions are in use. The analytics event database does not store the audio or video files on your device, your lyrics, your name, or your email address. The API host and its infrastructure may also process your IP address and ordinary request/connection information when handling a network request. The app's analytics event payload does not intentionally include your IP address.

The app also requests a public version-information file from the A1 FAMILY MEDIA website when it checks whether an update is available. This request is used to display update information; it does not include your media files or lyrics. The website host may process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request headers, when responding.

There is currently no automated retention or deletion schedule for analytics event records. You can email a request to the contact below. Requests will be reviewed and handled manually where technically and legally possible; this is not an instant in-app deletion feature.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The A1 FAMILY MEDIA website and Android app display advertisements supplied by Adsterra. In the Android app, the home-page sponsored placement and the song-list banner load Adsterra advertising code inside WebViews. The website also loads Adsterra advertising code.

When an advertisement loads or is interacted with, Adsterra and its advertising partners may receive or collect information such as your IP address, browser or device information, cookies or similar identifiers, and ad-view or click information. The app's home advertisement WebView permits third-party cookies. These providers may use information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, personalisation or other purposes described in their own notices. Their processing is governed by their policies, not this policy. Review Adsterra's privacy policy and cookie information.

When you tap an eligible advertisement link in the app, it may open in your device's external browser. The advertiser's or destination website's privacy terms then apply to your visit.

5. Permissions and your choices

Media permissions are used for the player features you choose to use. You can change app permissions through Android Settings. You can also stop using the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling the app removes its app-local preferences from the device, but does not by itself delete analytics events already received by the cloud service or information held by advertising providers.

6. Children's privacy

A1 FAMILY MEDIA is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed as a service for children. Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern about a child's use of the app can contact us using the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, website, advertising or analytics practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

8. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by A1 FAMILY MEDIA, email dailywalk.app1@gmail.com. Please describe your request and identify the app or service you used. If the app displays an installation reference in Privacy Choices, include that reference so we can locate the associated analytics event more accurately. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information by email.
