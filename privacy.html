About the app

A1 FAMILY MEDIA is an Android music and video player designed to help people browse and play media stored on their devices.

Access to media

The app may request Android media permissions so that it can find and play audio and video selected from the device's local media library. Grant only the permissions needed for the features you choose to use. You can review or change app permissions in your Android device settings.

Advertising and third-party services

This website displays third-party advertising supplied by Adsterra. When an ad is requested or displayed, your browser may connect to Adsterra and its advertising partners. Those providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser/device information, and ad interaction data under their own privacy terms. Their use of information is governed by their policies. The Android app itself does not embed this website JavaScript ad tag.

We do not claim that third-party advertising is free of tracking. For details about Adsterra's processing, review Adsterra's privacy policy.

Website download and installation analytics

If enabled, the website uses a random identifier stored in your browser to distinguish a first-time visitor or first-time APK download from repeat activity in the same browser profile. The Android app uses a random installation identifier to record its first launch. These identifiers are not your name or email address. The analytics service stores a one-way salted hash of each identifier in a private Google Sheet and may send the app owner an email when a new unique download or first launch is recorded. A different browser, cleared browser storage, another device, or reinstalling the app may be counted separately. People sharing one browser profile may be counted together. We do not use IP-address fingerprinting for this count. Analytics will only operate after the tracking service has been configured and deployed.

Children's privacy

The final release policy will explain any age-related requirements and the handling of information relating to children, where applicable.

Policy updates

This page will be updated when the app's release configuration and data practices have been verified. Please check this page again for the policy that applies to the version you use.
