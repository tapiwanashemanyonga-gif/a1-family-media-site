## Product truth — keep every post accurate
A1 FAMILY MEDIA is an Android music and video player. Its lyrics editor lets a user write or paste their own lyrics for the selected song, save them locally on the device for that track, and export them as a PDF. Do not claim automatic/AI lyric generation, automatic online lyric fetching, cloud sync, publishing/distribution, collaboration, or guaranteed copyright protection.

## Core message
Enjoy your music and videos. When inspiration comes, write or paste your own lyrics, save them on your device and export a PDF.

## 3 content pillars
1. **Songwriters / artists:** capturing ideas, keeping lyrics organised with the selected track, PDF export.
2. **Music lovers:** local music and video playback, playlists, favourites, shuffle/repeat, plus the lyrics editor.
3. **Everyday creators:** a simple Android app that combines listening, watching and personal lyric notes.

## Posting rules
- Record in vertical 9:16; show the actual app, not a mockup pretending to be the app.
- Hook viewers immediately; use concise on-screen captions and readable text.
- Use original audio or sounds you are allowed to use, especially for commercial promotion.
- Ask a question to invite genuine replies.
- Put the creator landing page in the profile/bio link where supported.
- Never promise that the app is on Google Play; the current download is an official GitHub APK.
- Be transparent that Android may require the user to approve installation from their browser or file manager.
- Do not buy followers, spam groups, mass-message strangers, or copy/paste identical posts everywhere.
